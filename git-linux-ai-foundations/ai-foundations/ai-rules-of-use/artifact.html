Three lanes at the prompt boundary

Before anything goes into a prompt, put it in a lane. Share: material your program or employer permits for that approved tool and account, with no secrets, personal data or confidential business details. Redact first: useful material with a secret or personal detail inside. Never share: material that is secret or personal as a whole, such as private keys or customer records.

Treat anything you paste as having left your control.

Your task

  1. Sort each item into a lane. The item to sort is named under Item and outlined in the model.
  2. Press the lane it belongs in: Share, Redact first or Never share.
  3. Read the feedback, then sort the next item until all six are in a lane.

Redact, don't discard

Redacting keeps what helps and replaces what harms with a placeholder such as <TOKEN>. Error codes and file and line numbers usually help; tokens, passwords and personal details do not.

You also own the result. Submit code an assistant proposed, and you answer for it as if you wrote it. Use only tools your program or employer approves.

Your task

  1. Under Parts of the error output, press each part that must be redacted. Press a part again to undo.
  2. Read what would be pasted in the model.
  3. Press Check redaction.

Recap

  • Sort before you paste: share, redact first, or never share.
  • Redact the secret, keep the useful part: replace values with placeholders such as <TOKEN>.
  • Treat anything you paste as out of your control.
  • You answer for AI-proposed code you submit, and you use only approved tools.
Cheat sheet
Sharematerial your program or employer permits for the approved tool: no secrets, personal data or confidential business details
Redact firsterrors and logs with a token, password or email inside
Never shareprivate keys, password files, customer or personal records
PlaceholderNAME=<NAME>: keep the name, replace the value
