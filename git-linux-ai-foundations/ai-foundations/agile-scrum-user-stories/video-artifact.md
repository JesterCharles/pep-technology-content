## What it covers

The narration walks one sprint in order: the Product Owner's ordered product backlog, Sprint Planning pulling the top items into a Sprint backlog for a Sprint goal, Sprints of one to four weeks, a Daily Scrum of no more than 15 minutes, the Sprint Review where stakeholders see the increment, and the Sprint Retrospective on process.

## Gaps

Two of the page's three objectives are missing: user stories (o2) and testable acceptance criteria (o3).
Pair it with the page's story builder and criteria checks; the alternate below covers user stories.

## Cautions

- Goes beyond the page: the 3-5-3 count (three accountabilities, five events including the Sprint, three artifacts), the artifacts' commitments and the increment.
- Two small departures from the 2020 Scrum Guide wording the page follows: "the scrum team gathers for the daily scrum" (the guide: an event for the Developers) and "prioritized wish list" (the guide: ordered).
